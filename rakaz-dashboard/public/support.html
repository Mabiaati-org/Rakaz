Support

Help for the Rakaz Parent and Rakaz Driver apps. We are happy to assist with accounts, trips, and school transport questions.

How to reach us

  • In the Parent app: open the Help tab to call, message on WhatsApp, or send a support request.
  • In the Driver app: open the menu and use Call Administration, or ask your transport office for a password reset.
  • Privacy policy: admin.rakaz.autos/privacy.html

Accounts

There is no public sign-up. Parent and driver accounts are created by the transport administration. If you cannot sign in, contact your school transport office and ask them to reset your password from the Rakaz admin dashboard.

App Store / Play Store reviewers

Reviewer credentials are provided in App Review Information for each app submission. Background location in the Driver app is used only during an active trip and during the admin-defined work schedule, as described in the review notes.

الحسابات

لا يوجد تسجيل عام. حسابات أولياء الأمور والسائقين تصدرها إدارة النقل. إذا تعذّر الدخول، تواصل مع إدارة النقل في مدرستك لطلب كلمة مرور جديدة من لوحة ركاز.
